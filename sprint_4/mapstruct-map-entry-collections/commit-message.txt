== Problem ==

substantial implementation volume, but most agents found the central Map-as-Map.Entry model and
then inherited the remaining behavior from MapStruct's existing container machinery. The false-
positive panels also exposed two incomplete implementation classes: globally exempting Map from
iterable/non-iterable validation admitted the separate Stream mapping family, and one generated
entry local collided with a legal @Context parameter.

The final iteration needed to close those public contract boundaries without expanding into
reference-failing edge cases, changing the correct reference solution, or turning the task into a
zero-pass conjunction.

== Analysis ==
The accepted result confirms that positive type-matrix growth was not durable difficulty. Once a
fresh solver recognized Map.Entry as the element model, arrays and lower bounds became checklist
items. Preserving MapStruct's separate Stream family added a repository-native architectural fork:
the implementation had to classify exactly the supported Map/iterable-or-array pairs instead of
weakening a surrounding validation guard.

That boundary separated six broad implementations while remaining explicit, regression-grounded,
and reference-green. The context case closed the other confirmed false-positive class through
observable behavior without distorting the pass rate. The two genuine passers and unanimous final
approval show that the contract is difficult because of precise subsystem integration rather than
ambiguity or hidden implementation preferences.
